# Day 000 — Start Here

This repository is a public record of an approximately six-month learning roadmap for becoming a stronger full-stack developer through deliberate practice. The numbered days show curriculum order, not calendar dates or deadlines.

## The rule

Every lesson must produce evidence. Evidence can be code, a test, a diagram, a reference note, a debugging write-up, or a deployed improvement. Watching a lesson alone does not count.

## First exercise

Write three sentences:

1. Why do I want to become a full-stack developer?
2. What kind of software do I want to be able to build?
3. What would make the final curriculum milestone feel successful even if I do not get a job immediately?
