# Complete Study Plan

This is the full resource-led execution plan for [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md). It currently contains 152 numbered lessons because that is what remained after grouping related concepts, removing redundant daily assignments, and adding milestone work where integration is educationally useful.

The workload target is approximately 360–480 focused hours over roughly six months. A numbered **Day** is a curriculum identifier—not a calendar day, study session, or fixed timebox.

## Lesson types

- **Learn:** complete the bounded reading and the resource’s own examples, exercises, or knowledge checks. Do not invent another project.
- **Milestone:** build something meaningful from a recent cluster of lessons, with minimal step-by-step guidance.
- **Integration:** connect capabilities in a real project and debug the boundaries between them.
- **Review:** retrieve from memory, repair gaps, and decide whether to proceed or spend another session.

For Learn lessons, “ready” means the resource exercises are complete and the concept can be explained in your own words. Substantial portfolio evidence belongs mainly to Milestone and Integration lessons.

## Review rhythm

Milestones replace arbitrary daily homework. After each milestone, ask:

1. What can I now explain and reproduce without the resource?
2. Which failure can I diagnose that I could not diagnose before?
3. What evidence came from the project rather than from time spent?
4. What should I revisit before the next cluster?
5. Which upcoming Learn lessons are familiar enough to combine?

The curriculum is complete when the integrated evidence is credible—not when every link has merely been opened.
